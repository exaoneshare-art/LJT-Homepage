I am a first-year PhD candidate in the NLP Group at [HKUST](https://www.ust.hk/), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.ust.hk/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, particularly in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
